LEVEL 1-1 "Ancient road across the Gobi" - theme: OPENNESS, long sight lines. Time: one hour after dawn; the player travels from the LEFT (west) to the RIGHT (east) into a low golden morning sun that sits beyond the right edge, so every object throws a long shadow toward the left.

A wide open basin of dark grey-brown gravel gobi, almost all of it walkable, crossed by a pale half-buried caravan road with faint cart ruts that snakes from left to right. The basin is bounded by terrain only: tall wind-sculpted sand dunes with sharp crests along the top (north) and the right (east), and an eroded dry-wash scarp along the bottom (south).

LEFT EDGE: a ruined rammed-earth post station in a saddle between two low rocky hills, with a broken gate frame that the road passes through - badge S.
LEFT THIRD: a long broken Han-dynasty rammed-earth frontier wall (ochre layered earth, half buried in sand) runs from top to bottom across the basin. It has three ways through: the main gap where the road passes; a collapsed breach further up with a stump of a corner watchtower (a cyan cache square inside the corner); and its lower end dying out into a dry riverbed that one can walk around.
Just right of the main gap: a flat dark gravel field with a few low wide boulders in clusters - badge 1.
MIDDLE: a shallow meandering dry riverbed with pale cracked mud and a few dead poplar trees on its banks crosses diagonally from upper right to lower left; the road fords it. The riverbed is a sunken flank route.
UPPER MIDDLE: a group of three wind-carved sandstone yardang mounds - ROUNDED whale-back / upturned ship-hull shapes with a blunt upwind head and a long tapering tail (NOT flat-topped mesas or slabs), 3.5 m, 6 m and 9 m tall, muted dusty red, all aligned the same diagonal direction, with a sand ramp onto the lowest one - badge 2.
CENTRE RIGHT, on the highest ground: a plain low square rammed-earth terrace about 6 m tall (battered earth walls, a simple crenellated parapet, NOT a stepped pyramid) with one earth ramp on its left side and one on its right side, crowned by a beacon tower with a tall column of grey-brown signal smoke leaning in the wind and a small orange fire on top - the dominant landmark, badge 3.
LOWER RIGHT: a sunken sand hollow ringed by dunes with wrecked caravan carts, cargo piles, a tattered indigo market awning and camel bones - badge 4. A low wall of yellow dust creeps over the dunes at the far right (a sandstorm arrives from the east).
FAR RIGHT EDGE, in a notch between two dunes where the road leaves: a small stone portal shrine with a soft teal glow - badge E.
LOWER MIDDLE: open gravel plain with one lone yardang rock sheltering abandoned cargo (the second cyan cache square, with an orange diamond next to it).
FAR TOP beyond the dunes: pale layered mesas and a giant seated Buddha statue silhouette dissolving into warm haze.

Routes: GOLD main route visits all four strongholds: S - gap - 1 - across the ford - up to 2 - then down to the platform ramp - 3 - down the far ramp - 4 - E. WHITE route A: from S through the upper breach past the corner watchtower cache - joins at 2. WHITE route B: from 1 down along the right side of the wall to its lower end - along the riverbed - lower gravel plain past the lone rock cache - joins at 4.
Palette: 60% dark warm-grey gravel and pale sand, 30% ochre rammed earth and red sandstone, under 10% saturated accents (beacon fire, teal portal, indigo awning).
