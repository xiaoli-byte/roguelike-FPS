LEVEL 1-A "Thousand-Buddha Cliff" (alternate map) - theme: VERTICAL LAYERS, three walkable tiers. Time: mid-afternoon, golden sun from the lower left lighting the cliff face, the riverbed below partly in cool shadow.

A long crescent-shaped ochre sandstone cliff on the outside of a wide dry river bend, facing the viewer, honeycombed with hundreds of dark cave niches in rows (like the Mogao grottoes). Three tiers, all walkable: the cliff FOOT (river terrace), a MID gallery 8 m up made of wooden eaves, balconies and plank walkways hugging the niches, and the cliff TOP 18 m up. In the centre of the cliff a tall nine-storey red timber pavilion facade climbs the whole cliff over a giant seated Buddha niche - it is the vertical connector between all three tiers. Other connectors: a rubble ramp from a cliff collapse at the left end, a big sand drift piled against the cliff reaching the mid gallery, and a switchback rock-cut stair at the right end. On the cliff top: a walled stupa courtyard with a slender pagoda (the dominant landmark) and a row of small white stupas along the rim; behind it a flat gravel plateau fading into haze. Below the cliff: a broad braided dry riverbed of pale gravel with one sand islet holding a wrecked caravan (carts, cargo, a toppled barricade), and on the near bank a grove of golden poplars and a ruined ferry terrace.

The level is a climb from the riverbed to the cliff top; the badges are numbered in climbing order.
BOTTOM LEFT, on the near bank ferry terrace: badge S, looking across the river at the whole cliff.
Sand islet with the caravan wreck in the riverbed: badge 1.
Foot plaza in front of the nine-storey facade: badge 2.
Left part of the mid gallery (wooden walkways and eaves): badge 3.
Cliff-top stupa courtyard: badge 4.
Cliff top far right, beside the last white stupa: small stone portal shrine with teal glow - badge E.
Two cyan cache squares: a sealed library cave at the far left end of the mid gallery, and a skeleton with treasure on the near bank (orange diamond beside it).
Enemy entry triangles: on the cliff rim (jumping down), in the upstream riverbed, behind the poplar grove, at gallery doorways, and one in the sky near the pagoda (a flying enemy).

Routes: GOLD main route visits all four strongholds from bottom to top: S - into the riverbed - 1 (islet) - 2 (foot plaza) - up inside the nine-storey facade to the mid gallery - 3 - up the rubble ramp at the left end to the top - 4 - along the rim past the white stupas - E. WHITE route A: from 1 to the switchback stair at the right end - up to the cliff top - joins at E and 4 (high shortcut). WHITE route B: from the riverbed up the sand drift ramp straight to the mid gallery - joins at 3.
Palette: 60% warm ochre cliff and pale river gravel, 30% weathered red-brown timber and cool shadow, under 10% accents (gilded pagoda finial, teal portal, golden poplars).
