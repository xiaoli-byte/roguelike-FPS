LEVEL 1-4 "Singing-Sand Mountain and the Crescent Spring" - the chapter BOSS arena, theme: OPEN sky plus SUN AND SHADE. Time: fierce late-morning sun from the upper left, hard-edged shadows.

An open-air sand hollow ringed on all sides by towering knife-edge mega-dunes (30 to 40 m, sharp sinuous crests, one sunlit face and one shadowed slip face each), the tallest mass along the bottom (south). In the hollow lies a crescent-shaped spring: a slim turquoise crescent of water with its convex side toward the top of the image, the ONLY cool colour in the picture, edged with reeds. In the embrace of the crescent on its lower (south) bank stands a Chinese pavilion complex: a two-storey pavilion with a half-collapsed roof, a paved courtyard, an open cloister, and a grove of poplar trees casting shade. On the upper (north) shore: two small barchan dunes like islands (climbable from their gentle side), a ruined low shrine wall, and dead willow trunks. A walkable loop of firm sand circles the whole spring.

UPPER LEFT: a timber viewing platform high on a dune saddle with lanterns and a merchant tent (safe camp) and a zigzag boardwalk stair descending into the hollow - badge S.
CENTRE of the spring: a red badge with letter B = the boss, a drought demon rising from the water with a burning sun-disc halo behind her head (small, stylised).
On the north shore where the stair arrives: yellow badge 1 = player entry into the arena.
Shade is marked as soft blue-violet shadow shapes: under the pavilion and cloister (deep shade), behind the two barchan dunes, under the poplars.
Four small MAGENTA swirls in the sand around the spring = places where sand wraiths are summoned.
Three small upward chevrons = high perches: the two barchan tops and the upper terrace of the pavilion.
A GOLD dashed loop with arrowheads circles the spring = the kiting circuit; WHITE dashed spurs lead from the loop into each shade zone.
Green badge E at the pavilion courtyard = exit that appears after victory.
The water level is hinted in three thin concentric crescent outlines inside the spring (full, half drained, dry cracked bed) to show the three boss phases.
Far beyond the dune ring: endless pale dune sea dissolving in hot haze.

Palette: 65% warm pale sand, 25% cool violet shadow and weathered grey timber, under 10% accents (turquoise spring, red pavilion columns, boss sun-disc).
