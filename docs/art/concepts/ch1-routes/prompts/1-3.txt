LEVEL 1-3 "Sand-drowned city" - theme: CITY with three parallel layers (wall-top walk 8 m, rooftops and sand terraces 4 m, streets 0 m). Time: early afternoon, sun high from the upper left, deep cool shadows in the streets, bright warm sand cascades.

An ancient Silk Road oasis city half swallowed by a giant transverse sand dune. The city wall is an irregular rounded trapezoid of thick rammed earth with a walkable top, crenellations, corner towers and ramps. The huge dune fills the UPPER LEFT, has buried a third of the city, and pours over the broken wall into the streets in fan-shaped sand slopes that reach roof level. Inside: clusters of flat-roofed adobe houses buried to their eaves (walkable roofs connected by sand drifts and plank bridges), two-storey timber shop houses along a bending main street, and in the middle the temple that still stands clear of the sand.

UPPER LEFT, on the dune crest outside and above the wall: badge S, overlooking the whole city.
Where the sand pours through the breached north-west gate, a wide sand slope between broken gate towers: badge 1.
UPPER MIDDLE: the long main street bending to the right, lined by shop houses, with a parallel rooftop route and the wall-top walk above it: badge 2.
RIGHT: a sunken market square with a roofed well pavilion in its centre, stalls with faded awnings, steps down on three sides: badge 3.
CENTRE-BOTTOM: the temple forecourt, a large paved court with a huge seated stone colossus on a plinth and two-storey open cloisters on both sides: badge 4.
BOTTOM CENTRE, behind the forecourt: a tall seven-tier pagoda, the dominant landmark, with a small walled courtyard beside it holding a safe supply camp (merchant tent, four braziers, lanterns) marked with a blue tent icon.
BOTTOM RIGHT, behind the pagoda: the ground has collapsed into a round sinkhole pit with a spiral ramp down to a dark underground canal opening: badge E.
Two cyan cache squares: one on the upper wall-top near a ramp, one among buried houses at the lower left (orange diamond beside it).
Outside the wall: a dry moat and pale dune field fading into haze.

Routes: GOLD main route S - down the sand slope - 1 - 2 - 3 - 4 - past the pagoda - E. WHITE route A: along the wall-top walk from the breach round to the market. WHITE route B: across the buried rooftops at the left straight down to the temple forecourt.

Magenta entry triangles sit only at real openings: on the dune where sand overtops the wall, at the open south gate, at a collapsed breach in the right-hand wall, on the wall-top walk and on a rooftop (jumping down).
