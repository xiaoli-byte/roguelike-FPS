MAP 1-A 千佛崖 (Cliff of a Thousand Buddhas) — motif: VERTICAL layers (riverbed 0 m / cave-eave gallery +8 m / clifftop +18 m).
Mood: late-afternoon low sun from the south-west turns the long crescent cliff into a golden wall; the riverbed at its foot still lies in cool shadow; the temple pagoda spire on the clifftop is the single brightest point.
Light: sun low (about 33 degrees) from the south-west, warm gold on the cliff face, cool shadows in the riverbed, bright pale sky, a soft haze band along the river.
What the masses are: a long ochre sandstone cliff (like Mogao) honeycombed with cave openings and Buddha niches, with timber cave-eave galleries (red columns, grey roofs) along an 8 m rock terrace; a nine-storey timber pavilion facade against the cliff (stacked flying eaves, the classic nine-storey silhouette, about 35 m) housing a colossal seated Buddha, with an open columned hall at its foot; a timber stair on posts climbing the cliff beside it; a collapsed rubble slope at the west end; a zigzag rock-cut stair at the east end; on the clifftop a walled temple court with a pagoda; in front a wide dry braided riverbed with a sandbar where a caravan camp lies wrecked; a golden-leaved poplar grove on the near bank; at spawn a timber gate frame on an old ferry landing frames the view.
The cliff in the reference reads too low from afar: you MAY make the clifftop hinterland read as a higher, receding plateau and add distant mesas behind it as background, but keep the playable cliff face, terraces and openings where they are.
