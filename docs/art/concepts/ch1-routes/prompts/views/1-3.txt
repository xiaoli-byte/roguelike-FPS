MAP 1-3 沉沙古城 (City Sinking in Sand) — motif: CITY with three parallel layers (wall top +8 m / buried rooftops +4 m / streets).
Mood: high afternoon sun: deep cool shadows in the streets and alleys, sand cascades pouring over the north-west wall into the city glowing in the light; the temple pagoda always stands on the skyline.
Light: sun high (about 52 degrees) from the south-west, crisp shadows, bright sky, haze over the far side of the city.
What the masses are: an irregular rounded rammed-earth city wall with corner towers, gate towers and crenellations; a giant dune burying the north-west third of the city and spilling through the walls; two-storey timber shophouses with grey tile roofs and awnings along a long street; buried flat-roof mud-brick houses with only their roofs showing (walkable rooftops); a sunken market square with a timber well pavilion and canvas stalls; a temple forecourt with two-storey cloisters, a gatehouse, steles and incense burners, and at its north end an open-fronted timber hall housing a seated colossal Buddha; a multi-eave timber temple pagoda (the landmark, about 22 m); behind it a collapsed sinkhole with a spiral ramp down to a dark water channel (exit).
