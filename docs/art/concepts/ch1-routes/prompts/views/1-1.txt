MAP 1-1 古道穿越戈壁 (Ancient Road across the Gobi) — motif: OPEN.
Mood: one hour after dawn, travelling east into the rising sun: long shadows fall back toward the player, the beacon tower's smoke column rises straight up, backlit; on the eastern horizon a dark ochre sandstorm wall is building (low on the horizon, not covering the play area).
Light: low warm sun in the east (elevation about 19 degrees), golden rim light on every edge, cool grey-teal shadows, pale gold sky brightest near the sun.
What the masses are: Han-dynasty Great Wall remnants (long low rammed-earth walls with reed-layer bands, breached in places); a rammed-earth beacon tower standing on a flat-topped mesa with a crenellated parapet and brushwood piles (the landmark, about 20 m above the plain); wind-eroded clay yardang mounds; a dry riverbed; a wrecked caravan camp (broken carts, goods bales, canvas stall, barricades, a brazier); at spawn a ruined posthouse with its old timber gate; far east a stone portal shrine (exit altar) with a faint glow.

[spawn]
VIEW spawn: the very first look of the player, standing inside the ruined posthouse, looking east through its timber gate. The beacon tower on its mesa on the horizon must catch the eye first (backlit, smoke rising). The main road leads straight ahead.
[s1]
VIEW s1: approaching stronghold 1 (black gravel flat) from 32 m away: a wide gravel arena with scattered half-buried sandstone boulders as half cover; the Great Wall breach behind. Add 5 to 6 small desert monsters (jerboa-like critters and an armoured scorpion guard) in the arena so enemy readability against the ground can be judged: they must pop out from the background.
[s2]
VIEW s2: approaching stronghold 2 (yardang stone circle): low clay yardang mounds and a toppled column ruin; the beacon tower visible beyond. Add 5 to 6 small monsters (critters, a mirage toad, a sand wraith) in the arena, readable against the background.
[s3]
VIEW s3: approaching stronghold 3 (beacon tower mesa): the mesa with its earthen ramp and parapet, the tower towering above; a winged demon flying near the tower and a tomb-guardian beast charging down the ramp, readable against the sky and the mesa.
[s4]
VIEW s4: approaching stronghold 4 (wrecked caravan camp): carts, goods bales, a canvas stall, barricades; the sandstorm wall looming on the horizon behind. Add 6 to 8 monsters including two larger elite ones, readable.
[exit]
VIEW exit: approaching the exit: a tall stone portal shrine glowing softly, the brightest, warmest point of the frame; the sandstorm close behind.
