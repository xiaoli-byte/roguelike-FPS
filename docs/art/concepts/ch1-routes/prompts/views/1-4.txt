MAP 1-4 鸣沙山月牙泉 · 旱魃 (Singing Sand Mountain and the Crescent Spring, boss arena of the drought demon Hanba) — motif: OPEN BOWL, sun exposure versus shade.
Mood: blazing noon in an open sand bowl: the ring of knife-edged dunes is bright on one side and dark on the other; the crescent spring is the only cool colour in the frame; shadows ARE cover.
Light: sun high (about 46 degrees) from the north-west, harsh white-gold light, short hard shadows pointing south-east, heat shimmer over the sand, sky pale and almost white near the horizon.
What the masses are: a ring of huge dunes with sharp crests (40 m on the south); two crescent barchan dunes on the north shore; a crescent-shaped spring with reeds and a cracked dry rim; on the south shore a two-storey Tang timber pavilion (open ground floor with only red columns that the path runs through, an open balcony on the upper floor, part of the upper roof collapsed), a long open gallery (two rows of red columns, grey tile roof) running east to a small gatehouse, a stone-paved courtyard with steles, incense burners and the exit altar; white poplar trees (straight pale trunks, columnar yellow-green crowns) in rows west of the pavilion; a ruined shrine and dead trees on the north shore; a wooden lookout platform with a zigzag stair on the north-west dune.

[spawn]
VIEW spawn: first look from the wooden lookout platform on the north-west dune: the whole bowl below, the crescent spring (cool teal, focal point) with the pavilion on its south shore; the zigzag stair going down.
[sB]
VIEW sB: on the north shore at the arena entrance looking across the spring: the drought demon Hanba rises from the centre of the spring, a tall gaunt figure in tattered red robes with a blazing sun-disc halo behind her, the hottest brightest point; the pavilion, gallery and poplars behind across the water. She must read clearly against the background.
[exit]
VIEW exit: the paved courtyard behind the pavilion after victory: the exit altar glowing, poplars, the open ground floor of the pavilion and the shade of the long gallery.
