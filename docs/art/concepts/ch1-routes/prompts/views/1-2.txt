MAP 1-2 雅丹迷城 (Yardang Labyrinth) — motif: TIGHTENING maze of braided sand channels between yardangs.
Mood: windy late morning, the sun ahead-right of the direction of travel: blowing sand flows along the channels glowing in backlight; every yardang wall is warm orange on its lit face and cool on its shadowed face; the stupa spire and the rock arch are silhouettes in the sand haze.
Light: sun elevation about 33 degrees from the east-south-east, strong backlight with glowing sand streams, dusty air, sky pale and bright.
What the masses are: tall wind-carved clay yardangs with layered horizontal strata (reddish-brown masses), one shaped like a crouching lion; sand channels and passes; half-buried flat-roof houses whose roofs form a high path; a half-buried Buddhist stupa on a two-tier ring terrace raised on a 10 m pedestal, the compass landmark seen everywhere (about 30 m); a natural rock arch leading to the exit platform with a stone portal shrine; quicksand patches (slightly darker, wet-looking sand).
