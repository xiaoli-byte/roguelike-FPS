LEVEL 1-2 "Yardang maze city" - theme: TIGHTENING, short sight lines that open only at two places. Time: a windy mid-morning; the player travels from the LEFT to the RIGHT, the sun is ahead and to the right (beyond the upper right corner), so rock walls have one warm orange sunlit side and one cool violet shadow side, and long shadows fall toward the lower left.

A maze of long wind-carved red sandstone yardang ridges - blade-like rock walls 10 to 20 m tall with broad horizontal ochre and cream strata, whale-back and ship-hull shapes with blunt upwind heads and tapering tails, ALL elongated in the same left-to-right wind direction. Between them weave pale sandy channels of an ancient dry riverbed that split into three strands and rejoin, with narrow cross-gaps and sand saddles between the rock islands (a maze, not corridors: every open space has at least three ways out). A higher outer cliff with a notched skyline encloses the maze. Half-buried rooftops and a stupa spire of a sunken city poke out of the sand.

LEFT EDGE: a raised sand fan at the mouth of the riverbed, beside a sphinx-shaped yardang rock - badge S. From here three dark channel mouths are visible.
LEFT CENTRE: a delta-shaped open space where the riverbed splits into three channels, with a low rock islet and a wrecked caravan cart - badge 1.
UPPER CENTRE: a wide pale sand basin where the roofs of a buried quarter emerge: a chain of five flat adobe roofs like stepping stones (the houses are buried in sand up to their eaves: only roofs and parapets emerge, sand drifts ramp onto them, no doors or walls visible) and one dark-tiled hip roof with red gable, three subtle quicksand swirls between them - badge 2.
LOWER CENTRE-RIGHT: a round rammed-earth plaza with a half-buried pale stupa standing on two ring terraces with ramps, its tall gilded finial catching the sun - the tallest landmark, badge 3.
RIGHT: the channels converge in a V toward a natural rock arch in the last wall - badge 4. Through and beyond the arch: a terrace with a small stone portal shrine - badge E - overlooking a hazy lowland with the faint silhouette of a walled city and a pagoda.
Two cyan cache squares: a rock niche in the upper channel wall, and a painted Buddhist grotto niche in the lower channel wall (with an orange diamond beside it).
Low routes on sand; a HIGH route over two low whale-back rocks with sand ramps and across the rooftops.
Atmosphere: streams of wind-blown sand flow along the channels and glow in the backlight; thin sand veils spill from cliff tops; the valley floor sits in luminous dust while cliff tops stay clear.

Routes: GOLD main route visits all four strongholds: S - 1 - upper channel - 2 - over a sand saddle - 3 - 4 - through the arch - E. WHITE route A: from 1 straight along the middle channel to 3 (shortcut). WHITE route B: from S along the lower channel past the grotto cache - joins at 4.
Between 2 and 3 there is a clearly painted sand saddle: a wide gap through the rock ridge filled with a smooth pale sand ramp. Route lines always run on the sand channels, never across the top of a rock ridge.
Palette: 60% pale beige sand and dusty air, 30% red-ochre banded rock, under 10% accents (gilded finial, teal portal).
