Hand-painted stylized game LEVEL ROUTE CONCEPT ART: a bird's-eye oblique view (camera high, looking about 50 degrees down) showing one ENTIRE open level from edge to edge, like an illustrated level-design map painted by a senior environment concept artist. Chunky simplified shapes, flat colour planes with two or three tone steps, clean silhouettes, no fine cracks, no photorealism, no people, no UI frame, no logo, no title. Clear readable terrain with strong low-angle light, long cast shadows that carve the ground, layered warm haze making far things paler, sky not visible or only a thin strip. Calm low-saturation ground; the brightest saturated accents are reserved for the annotation.

ROUTE ANNOTATION painted cleanly on top of the terrain (this is a design document, the annotation must be crisp and readable):
- a bold dashed GOLD line with arrowheads = the main route, passing through the numbered badges in order;
- thinner dashed WHITE lines with arrowheads = alternative flank routes that leave and rejoin the main route;
- round badges with a dark outline: white badge with letter S = player spawn; yellow badges with digits 1, 2, 3, 4 = combat strongholds; green badge with letter E = exit portal;
- small ORANGE diamonds = roadside ambush guards (4 of them, placed on the paths between badges);
- small MAGENTA triangles each with a short magenta arrow pointing into the level = enemy entry points (6 of them, at the edges: behind dunes, in dry channels, on rock tops);
- small CYAN squares = optional treasure caches (2 of them, off the main route).
No other text, no legend box, no labels besides the badge characters.
