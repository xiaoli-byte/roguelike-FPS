Repaint image 1 as a finished stylized game environment concept key frame.
STRICT RULES: keep exactly the same camera, horizon line and terrain shapes, and the position, size and silhouette of every building and structure in image 1. Do not move, add or remove buildings, do not change the scale of anything. Change only materials, light, colour and atmosphere. Background peaks may be repainted as softer layered snow mountains. Keep the bowl-shaped snow cirque, the pyramid-shaped sacred mountain seen through the notch, the lake outline, the small round island in the centre and the flat gravel beach in the foreground.
One planned addition is allowed: on the far left cirque wall the snow slope becomes a glacier tongue made of a few big faceted pale blue-white ice planes.

WHAT THE SHAPES ARE: the thin dark posts on the shore are tall square grey stone pillars with two dark iron bands and a pale unlit crystal on top; the island is a low rock with a round three-step stone altar holding a faint cyan-gold glow; the small piles on the shore are low mounds of flat stones with a few five-colour prayer flags.

LIGHT (6:3:1): first light of dawn: only the top third of the sacred mountain is lit warm gold; the whole cirque, lake and shore are in even, soft, cool shade (a narrow value range). Thin low mist floats on the lake and at the foot of the slopes. The mirror-calm lake reflects the mountain.
COLOUR (6:3:1): about 60% neutral cool grey snow shade and grey scree (low saturation, no blue-violet cast); about 30% milky pale jade-grey water and white snow; under 10%: the gold summit and its reflection, the cyan-gold altar glow, tiny prayer flags.
STYLE: stylized hand-painted 3D game environment, thick simple shapes, flat painted colour blocks, large quiet areas, smooth surfaces, no fine cracks, no carvings, no individual grass blades or flowers, no text, no labels, no people, no creatures.
