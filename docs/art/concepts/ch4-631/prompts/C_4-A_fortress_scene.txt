Stylized hand-painted 3D game environment concept art, 16:9 key frame for an open-world first-person shooter level, camera at eye height on a snowy valley meadow. Original world, a mountain fortress-monastery built up a steep rocky spur in the eastern Tibetan highlands, late afternoon after light snow.

COMPOSITION, diagonals plus an S-curve, strict 6:3:1 hierarchy:
- 60% BIG PLANES: a huge rocky mountain spur rises on the right half. The fortress climbs it as stacked massive white wall blocks with inward-sloping (battered) walls, like big simple boxes stepping up the rock, flat terraces between them. Behind, layered snow ridges fade into haze, each layer lighter. A snow-dusted pale straw meadow fills the foreground and middle ground. The sky is the brightest area, top left.
- 30% LINES: two long zigzag ramps with low parapets climb the white walls as strong diagonals. A stone road curves in an S from the foreground, across a small stone bridge over a half-frozen stream, to the fortress gate at the foot of the spur. A long low wall of flat stacked stones runs beside the road.
- 10% POINTS: at the very top, ONE dark red hall block with a small gilded roof, catching the last warm sunlight against the pale sky (the single anchor). Rows of identical small black trapezoid windows. A few tiny five-colour prayer flag strings.
- Left middle ground: a small village of five flat-roofed stone houses with firewood stacks. Far left on a separate ridge: an old square watchtower, half collapsed on one side (secondary landmark, smaller and hazier).

LIGHT: one warm low sun from the left; lit faces of the white walls are warm cream, shadow faces cool blue-grey, never black; large continuous shadow masses on the spur. A thin mist band lies at the base of the spur, separating the village from the fortress. Background values stay in a narrow calm band.

COLOUR: about 60% low-saturation grey rock, blue-grey snow shade, pale straw meadow; about 30% white walls, dark ochre-red bands, dark timber; under 10% high saturation: the red hall and gold roof, prayer flags.

STYLE RULES: thick simple shapes, big clean colour blocks, chunky stylized proportions, smooth painted surfaces, no fine cracks, no carvings, no ornament, no photo texture, no text, no people, no creatures, no tall thin poles in the middle distance. Readable at thumbnail size. The fortress must clearly read as built from a modular kit: three or four repeated wall block sizes, identical window frames, straight ramp segments, parapet strips.
