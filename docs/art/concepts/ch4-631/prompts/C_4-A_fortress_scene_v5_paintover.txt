Image 1 is a 3D blockout render of a planned game level, a mountain fortress-monastery, seen from the player's spawn point on a snowy valley meadow. Image 2 is its modular kit sheet: every wall, ramp and house must look built from these pieces.

Repaint image 1 as a finished stylized game environment concept key frame.
STRICT RULES: keep exactly the same camera, horizon line and terrain shapes, and the position, size and silhouette of every building and structure in image 1. Do not move, add or remove buildings, do not change the scale of anything. Change only materials, light, colour and atmosphere. Background peaks may be repainted as softer layered snow mountains. Do not make the fortress bigger or taller.

WHAT THE SHAPES ARE: right half: stacked white battered (inward-sloping) wall blocks climb a rocky spur, each with an ochre-red band along the top and rows of small black trapezoid windows; the thin diagonal strips between them are stone ramps with low white parapets (the player route, switchback by switchback); at the very top the dark red hall block with a small gold roof. The spur itself is big simple faceted grey rock planes with smooth snow on the flatter parts. Left: three flat-roofed village houses (grey stone ground floor, white upper floor, ochre band) with firewood stacks and a long low wall of flat stones. Foreground: a snow-covered meadow with soft patches of pale straw; a dirt road runs from the lower right toward a small stone bridge over a half-frozen stream and the fortress gate.

LIGHT (6:3:1): one warm low sun from the left; lit wall faces warm cream, shadow faces cool blue-grey, never black; big continuous shadow masses on the spur; a thin mist band at the foot of the spur and the background mountains fading into haze. The sky is the brightest area.
COLOUR (6:3:1): about 60% neutral cool light-grey snow shade (no lavender or purple), grey rock, pale straw; about 30% white walls, ochre-red bands, dark timber; under 10%: the dark red hall with the gold roof and a few tiny five-colour prayer flags.
STYLE: stylized hand-painted 3D game environment, thick simple shapes, flat painted colour blocks, large quiet areas, smooth surfaces, no fine cracks, no carvings, no individual grass blades or flowers, no text, no labels, no people, no creatures.
