Stylized hand-painted 3D game environment concept art, 16:9 key frame for a first-person shooter, camera at eye height on a high slope looking down into a canyon. Original world, Tibetan-Qiang highland river gorge, late afternoon.

COMPOSITION, strict 6:3:1 hierarchy:
- 60% BIG PLANES: two steep canyon walls form a V that frames the view. The left near slope is dark and in shadow. The right side is one huge, simple, faceted rock cliff plane. Behind, snow mountains recede in four layers into pale haze, each layer lighter and flatter. The sky is the brightest area of the image.
- 30% LINES: below, a Y-shaped river confluence: two milky grey-turquoise rivers meet in a deep stone trench and flow toward the viewer. An iron chain suspension bridge with plank deck crosses the main river in the lower middle as one long sagging curve. A wooden plank walkway is pinned to the right cliff face and climbs diagonally. Thin zigzag mule paths on the slopes.
- 10% POINTS: ONE focal landmark: a tall square white stone watchtower with an ochre-red band and black trapezoid windows, standing on the wedge-shaped rocky tongue of land between the two rivers, on the right third line; its top catches warm sunlight against a darker misty cliff behind it. One upper corner is broken off (asymmetric silhouette).
- Left middle ground: terraced barley fields step down a gentle slope, with five white flat-roofed stone village houses (simple boxes, ochre band under the roof, small roof terraces).
- Foreground bottom-left: a flat stone platform with a low dry-stone wall, dark and simple, not cluttered.

LIGHT: one warm low sun from the upper right, long clean shadow masses; shadow sides are cool blue-grey, never black. A low mist band lies in the canyon bottom and in front of the far cliff, separating the depth layers. Background values stay in a narrow calm band.

COLOUR: about 60% low-saturation grey-brown rock and cool blue-grey; about 30% white walls, ochre-red timber, muted green terraces, milky river; under 10% high saturation: a few tiny five-colour prayer flag strings and one small cyan-gold glowing altar at the near bridgehead.

STYLE RULES: thick simple shapes, big clean colour blocks, chunky stylized proportions, smooth painted surfaces, no fine cracks, no carvings, no dense foliage, no photo texture, no text, no people, no creatures, no tall thin poles in the middle distance. Readable at thumbnail size. Everything must look buildable from a modular 3D kit: repeated house blocks, straight terrace wall segments, straight walkway segments, one chain bridge.
