Stylized hand-painted 3D game environment concept art, 16:9 key frame for a first-person shooter boss arena, camera at eye height on the south shore of a lake. Original world, high-altitude glacial lake on the Tibetan plateau, first light of dawn.

COMPOSITION, centred with one asymmetric break, strict 6:3:1 hierarchy:
- 60% BIG PLANES: a round glacial cirque like a bowl: a calm, mirror-flat milky turquoise lake fills the lower middle; snow slopes and grey scree walls ring it. A sacred pyramid-shaped snow mountain rises in the far distance slightly right of centre, seen through a low notch in the moraine ridge, and is mirrored in the lake. Clear pale sky, brightest just around the mountain.
- 30% LINES: the curved grey gravel shoreline sweeps around the lake as a C-curve toward the notch. Three tall square stone pillars stand on the shore (one mid-left, one mid-right, one small far across the lake), each wrapped by two dark iron bands, with a faceted pale crystal on top. On the left a glacier tongue comes down in big simple faceted planes.
- 10% POINTS: only the top third of the mountain is lit gold by the first sun, while the whole basin is still in cool blue shade. A small round rocky island sits exactly in the centre of the lake with a low round stone altar on it and a faint cyan-gold glow.
- Near shore, kept to the left and right edges: two large rounded boulders and one low pile of flat stones with a few small five-colour prayer flag strings. The centre of the image stays empty and clean.

LIGHT: the gold light touches the peak only; the arena itself is in even, soft, cool shade (narrow value range, so creatures will stand out). Thin low mist floats on the lake surface and at the foot of the far slopes. The asymmetric break: glacier on the left, open notch right of centre.

COLOUR: about 60% cool low-saturation blue-grey snow shade and grey scree; about 30% milky turquoise water and white snow; under 10% high saturation: gold summit, prayer flags, cyan-gold altar glow.

STYLE RULES: thick simple shapes, big clean colour blocks, chunky stylized proportions, smooth painted surfaces, no fine cracks, no carvings, no photo texture, no text, no people, no creatures, no buildings other than the pillars and the altar. Readable at thumbnail size. Everything must look buildable from a small modular 3D kit plus terrain.
