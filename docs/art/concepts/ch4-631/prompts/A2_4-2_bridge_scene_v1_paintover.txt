WHAT THE SHAPES ARE:

- From the gatehouse the iron chain bridge spans the river trench to the far east bridgehead gatehouse (right of centre): one long sagging plank deck hung on dark iron chains, with two thin handrail chains on each side. No towers or supports in the middle of the span.
- The river runs at the bottom of a narrow trench cut into grey rock: calm milky grey-turquoise water, a few soft foam streaks. On the far east wall of the trench, the brown zigzag is a narrow wooden plank walkway fixed to the cliff on beams; paint it hugging the rock, never spanning the gap.
- Right: a squat thunder post (kit piece 12): round grey stone plinth with a short dark iron cage spike.
- Far left: the tongue of land between two rivers with the white square watchtower with a broken top corner; terraced slopes with a few white houses.
- All green surfaces are late-summer highland meadow on rolling slopes; the foreground is a calm meadow slope.

LIGHT (6:3:1): warm low late-afternoon sun from the left, behind the camera; long soft shadows; shadow sides cool blue-grey, never black; the far ridges fade into pale haze; a thin low mist band lies in the river trench. The sky is the brightest area.
COLOUR (6:3:1): about 60% muted low-saturation olive-grey and straw meadow with neutral grey rock; about 30% white walls, ochre-red bands, dark timber and iron, milky river; under 10%: a few tiny five-colour prayer flags and the warm-lit top of the far watchtower.
STYLE: stylized hand-painted 3D game environment, thick simple shapes, flat painted colour blocks, large quiet areas, smooth surfaces, no fine cracks, no carvings, no individual grass blades or flowers, no text, no labels, no people, no creatures.
