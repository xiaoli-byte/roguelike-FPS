WHAT THE SHAPES ARE: left middle ground, the terraced village slope: white flat-roofed stone houses with ochre-red top bands and a tall square white watchtower; low dry-stone terrace walls. Centre: a narrow river trench cut into grey rock, milky grey-turquoise water at its bottom; a wooden plank walkway on brackets climbs the right wall of the trench. Far centre, on the tongue of land between two rivers, the small square white watchtower with one broken top corner: this is the focal point. Right: the bridgehead gatehouse and a squat thunder post at the head of an iron chain bridge, the east bank's grey rock faces beyond. All the green surfaces are late-summer highland meadow on rolling slopes; the foreground is a quiet open meadow slope.

LIGHT (6:3:1): one warm low sun from the left, long soft shadows; shadow sides cool blue-grey, never black; the far ridges fade into pale haze, each layer paler; a thin low mist band lies in the river trench. The sky is the brightest area.
COLOUR (6:3:1): about 60% muted low-saturation olive-green and straw meadow with grey rock (far less saturated than the blockout's green); about 30% white walls, ochre-red bands, dark timber, milky river; under 10%: a few small five-colour prayer flags and the warm-lit top of the far watchtower.
STYLE: stylized hand-painted 3D game environment, thick simple shapes, flat painted colour blocks, large quiet areas, smooth surfaces, no fine cracks, no carvings, no individual grass blades or flowers, no text, no labels, no people, no creatures.
