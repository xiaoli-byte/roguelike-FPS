Chapter 2, alternative direction 'North Sea of Ice' (北冥冰海), from the opening of the Zhuangzi: 'In the northern darkness there is a fish named Kun; it changes into a bird named Peng'. A frozen polar sea at the end of the world: ancient wooden junks locked in the sea ice, whale-bone arches and cairns on the shore, glacier cliffs and ice caves, nomad felt tents. The great Kun stirs beneath the ice and the whole sea has frozen around it; its creatures and the drowned sailors froze into frost fiends. A floating frozen puffer fish (河豚), the kamikaze: a round grumpy pufferfish drifting in the air like a balloon, its skin covered with short thick ice spines, big angry eyes, a small pouting beak mouth, tiny fins fluttering; when it charges it inflates to twice its size and its spines glow aqua-cyan from the roots (weak spot: the whole body) before bursting into a ring of ice spines. Comic and explosive.
