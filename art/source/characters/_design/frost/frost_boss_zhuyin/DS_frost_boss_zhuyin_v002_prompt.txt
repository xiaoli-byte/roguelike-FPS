Original character design for a polished STYLIZED 3D first-person roguelite shooter, at the production
quality of Gunfire Reborn enemies. Strongly stylized, not realistic: simplified chunky shapes, exaggerated cartoon-like
proportions (big head and hands, compact body), bold readable silhouette, smooth sculpted forms with clean bevelled
edges, hair and fur as a few large carved clumps (never individual strands), clothing as thick simple folds, flat-ish
hand-painted textures with soft gradients and restrained edge wear, two or three dominant colour blocks plus one
glowing accent. No photorealistic skin, pores, anatomy detail, realistic fabric or semi-realistic painting.
Distinctly Chinese mythological fantasy. Memorable at 30 metres; one obvious glowing weak spot.

Chapter 2 'Kunlun Snow Ruins' (昆仑雪墟): the 'lower capital of the Heavenly Emperor' on Mount Kunlun from the Shan Hai Jing, a mountain city of white jade terraces, verdigris bronze ritual gates, frozen waterfalls and the frozen Jade Pool of the Queen Mother of the West. Zhuyin the Torch Dragon (烛阴) has closed its eyes and the mountain sank into an endless polar night; its beasts, guardians and pilgrims froze into frost fiends. Palette: glacier blue, moon white, pale jade green and verdigris bronze, with a little faded vermilion; the corrupting power is a cold cyan-violet aurora frost fire (polar-night ghost light) glowing from cracks and crystals.

Character: 烛阴, about 7 m tall. Chapter boss Zhuyin (烛阴), the Torch Dragon of the Shan Hai Jing: 'a human face and a serpent's body, red; opening its eyes makes day, closing them makes night; its breath is winter'. A colossal crimson serpent with NO legs, NO arms, NO claws and NO antlers or horns: only a huge stern ancient human face on a serpent's body. Heavy brows, a long flowing white beard and moustache like frozen waterfalls, two great eyes that glow white-gold like the sun, and a burning candle-pearl held between its lips (衔烛) that glows white-gold (weak spot). Its crimson scales are crusted with thick glacier ice along the back ridge and coils, and verdigris bronze ritual rings are clamped around its neck. Ancient, terrifying, majestic.

Stylization: cartoon game proportions in the same family as the desert roster (jerboa soldier, fire rat, sancai tomb beast): chunky simplified masses, oversized head and hands or paws, toy-like readable forms with clean bevelled edges, ice as a few big faceted crystal shapes (never thin needles or noisy frost texture), fur as a few large carved clumps. A boss: show only the head, neck and the first coils rising from the ground; the rest of the body disappears off the side of each view. It is a pure serpent with a human face: no limbs and no horns of any kind.

Output: one creature design sheet on a plain flat light grey background (#D6D6D6), no text, labels or
frames. Left half: a large three-quarter view in an aggressive action pose. Right half at the same scale: a FRONT view,
a SIDE view and a BACK view, each complete with margin. Even studio lighting, no cast shadows.
