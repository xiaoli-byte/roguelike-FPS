Chapter 2 'Kunlun Snow Ruins' (昆仑雪墟): the 'lower capital of the Heavenly Emperor' on Mount Kunlun from the Shan Hai Jing, a mountain city of white jade terraces, verdigris bronze ritual gates, frozen waterfalls and the frozen Jade Pool of the Queen Mother of the West. Zhuyin the Torch Dragon (烛阴) has closed its eyes and the mountain sank into an endless polar night; its beasts, guardians and pilgrims froze into frost fiends. Palette: glacier blue, moon white, pale jade green and verdigris bronze, with a little faded vermilion; the corrupting power is a cold cyan-violet aurora frost fire (polar-night ghost light) glowing from cracks and crystals.

Character: 雪狌狌, about 1.3 m tall. A pack-hunting snow monkey based on the xingxing (狌狌) of the Shan Hai Jing, 'shaped like a macaque with white ears, it crawls and walks like a man', modelled on the Chinese golden snub-nosed monkey of the snowy mountains (NOT a yeti, NOT a gorilla): a stocky, long-armed monkey with a bright sky-blue bare face, a tiny upturned snub nose, big expressive eyes and a wide mischievous grin, white ear tufts, a thick cape of long golden-white fur over the shoulders and back ending in icy blue tips, a short thick tail. A cluster of big faceted blue ice crystals grows out of its shoulders and spine, and one bright glowing cyan ice crystal is set in its forehead (weak spot). Scraps of a frozen pilgrim's vermilion prayer cord with small bronze bells around one wrist. Big hands with a clearly separate thumb and four fingers; hand-like feet with five toes. Cheeky, fast and annoying, dangerous in a pack.
