Original character design for a polished STYLIZED 3D first-person roguelite shooter, at the production

proportions (big head and hands, compact body), bold readable silhouette, smooth sculpted forms with clean bevelled

Distinctly Chinese mythological fantasy. Memorable at 30 metres; one obvious glowing weak spot.

Chapter 4 'Snowland Sky Road' (雪域天路): the high grassland plateau of western Sichuan and eastern Tibet, a wide green summer meadow basin ringed by snow peaks and a glacier, with a turquoise holy lake, a glacial river, stone watchtowers and colourful prayer flags. In myth the Yellow Emperor flayed Kui (夔), the one-legged thunder beast, to make a war drum; Kui's thunder soul slept in the holy lake and has now woken: thunderstorms sweep the plateau, and its marmots, yaks and the strange beasts of the Shan Hai Jing's Western Mountains are charged with corrupted thunder. Creature palette: plateau animal colours (greyish brown, cream white, rusty red, soot black) with weathered copper and bone accents; the corrupting thunder is a magenta-violet lightning glow with white-hot cores, showing as glowing zig-zag veins, crackling fur tips, glowing eyes and the weak spot (never the white-gold sun fire of chapter 1, the cyan-violet frost of chapter 2 or the lava orange of chapter 3). No people, no religious figures or symbols.

Character: 穷奇, about 1.3 m tall. Qiongqi (穷奇), one of the Four Fiends of Chinese myth, 'shaped like a tiger with wings', with the 'hedgehog-quill fur' of its Western Mountains description: a powerful winged tiger about 2.6 m long and 1.3 m at the shoulder, rusty orange-and-black tiger stripes, a big blocky head with a fierce snarl and fangs, two large feathered wings (dark brown with cream bands) spread for a gliding pounce, and a crest of stiff quill-like spines along the neck and back that crackles with magenta-violet lightning and glows (weak spot); violet glowing eyes, lightning veins along the wing bones. Apex predator. NOT a griffin: a real tiger body with wings.

Stylization: cartoon game proportions in the same family as the earlier rosters (jerboa soldier, fire rat, sancai tomb beast, huodou sighthound): chunky simplified masses, oversized heads and paws, toy-like readable forms with clean bevelled edges. Lightning is painted as OPAQUE glowing magenta-violet zig-zag veins and solid sculpted spark shapes on the body (never transparent bolts, particles, smoke or glow halos); fur, hair and feathers as a few large carved clumps.
