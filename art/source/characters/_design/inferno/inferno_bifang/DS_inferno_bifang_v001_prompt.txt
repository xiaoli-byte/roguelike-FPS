Chapter 3 'Zhurong's Fire Prison' (祝融火狱): the underground forge-palace and prison of Zhurong (祝融), the fire god of Chinese myth, deep under a volcano: black basalt pillars, black-iron halls, giant bronze ding cauldrons and furnaces, hanging chains, fire altars and rivers of lava. The god's fire has run wild and turned his smiths, jailers, forge-made constructs and fire beasts into fire fiends. Palette: black iron and basalt black, molten lava orange and vermilion lacquer red, with dark bronze; the corrupting fire is a deep molten lava-orange glow with yellow-hot cores, glowing from cracks, seams, mouths and vents (never the white-gold sun fire of chapter 1).

Character: 毕方, about 2 m tall. The bifang (毕方) of the Shan Hai Jing, 'shaped like a crane, with ONE SINGLE LEG, red markings on a dark ground and a white beak', whose appearance brings strange fires: a tall slender crane-like bird about 2 m tall standing on EXACTLY ONE long thin leg placed in the middle under its body, ending in one foot with three forward toes and one back toe; it has NO second leg anywhere, not even tucked up. A long S-curved neck, a long sharp white beak holding a glowing coal ember (衔火) that glows yellow-orange (weak spot), a crest of a few stiff vermilion flame-shaped feathers, deep blue-black plumage boldly patterned with vermilion stripes and spots, wings folded tightly against the body with flame-shaped vermilion tips, a short fan of stiff tail feathers. Elegant, eerie and sharp-eyed; it perches high on pillars and spears fire with its beak.

Stylization: cartoon game proportions in the same family as the desert and frost rosters (jerboa soldier, fire rat, sancai tomb beast, golden snub-nosed monkey): chunky simplified masses, oversized heads and hands or paws, toy-like readable forms with clean bevelled edges. Iron, basalt and bronze as a few big faceted plates with sparse painted wear; lava and fire painted as OPAQUE glowing cracks, seams and solid sculpted flame shapes (never transparent fire, smoke or particles); fur, hair and feathers as a few large carved clumps.
