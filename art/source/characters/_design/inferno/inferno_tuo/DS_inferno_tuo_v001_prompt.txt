Original character design for a polished STYLIZED 3D first-person roguelite shooter, at the production
quality of Gunfire Reborn enemies. Strongly stylized, not realistic: simplified chunky shapes, exaggerated cartoon-like
proportions (big head and hands, compact body), bold readable silhouette, smooth sculpted forms with clean bevelled
edges, hair and fur as a few large carved clumps (never individual strands), clothing as thick simple folds, flat-ish
hand-painted textures with soft gradients and restrained edge wear, two or three dominant colour blocks plus one
glowing accent. No photorealistic skin, pores, anatomy detail, realistic fabric or semi-realistic painting.
Distinctly Chinese mythological fantasy. Memorable at 30 metres; one obvious glowing weak spot.

Chapter 3 'Zhurong's Fire Prison' (祝融火狱): the underground forge-palace and prison of Zhurong (祝融), the fire god of Chinese myth, deep under a volcano: black basalt pillars, black-iron halls, giant bronze ding cauldrons and furnaces, hanging chains, fire altars and rivers of lava. The god's fire has run wild and turned his smiths, jailers, forge-made constructs and fire beasts into fire fiends. Palette: black iron and basalt black, molten lava orange and vermilion lacquer red, with dark bronze; the corrupting fire is a deep molten lava-orange glow with yellow-hot cores, glowing from cracks, seams, mouths and vents (never the white-gold sun fire of chapter 1).

Character: 火鼍, about 1.4 m tall. The tuo dragon (鼍龙) of the Black River in Journey to the West, reborn as a giant Chinese alligator (扬子鳄) that lives in the rivers of lava: a huge heavy alligator about 6 m long and 1.4 m tall with real crocodilian anatomy — broad blunt snout lined with jagged teeth, small eyes and nostrils high on the head, short sprawling legs with clawed feet, a long heavy tail with a double crest of plates. Its back is armoured with rows of big thick plates of cooled black basalt crust (like solidified lava rock), separated by glowing molten lava seams; a bright molten seam runs along its spine (weak spot). Its belly and the sides of the head are dark red-brown hide. Ancient, heavy and patient.

Stylization: cartoon game proportions in the same family as the desert and frost rosters (jerboa soldier, fire rat, sancai tomb beast, golden snub-nosed monkey): chunky simplified masses, oversized heads and hands or paws, toy-like readable forms with clean bevelled edges. Iron, basalt and bronze as a few big faceted plates with sparse painted wear; lava and fire painted as OPAQUE glowing cracks, seams and solid sculpted flame shapes (never transparent fire, smoke or particles); fur, hair and feathers as a few large carved clumps. Its defence is part of its own body: it holds NO shield and NO weapon.

Output: one creature design sheet on a plain flat light grey background (#D6D6D6), no text, labels or
frames. Left half: a large three-quarter view in an aggressive action pose. Right half at the same scale: a FRONT view,
a SIDE view and a BACK view, each complete with margin. Even studio lighting, no cast shadows.
