Character design sheet of ONE character only: a towering MOLTEN-COPPER DEMON WARLORD boss, a heavy two-handed greatsword swordsman, for a polished stylized 3D first-person roguelite shooter at the quality of Gunfire Reborn bosses. Stylized hand-painted 3D game character art: bold chunky silhouette, slightly exaggerated heroic proportions, broad clean faceted planes with bevelled edges, two or three dominant colour blocks, soft painted gradients and a few restrained edge accents; East-Asian fantasy motifs simplified into recognizable medium-scale shapes; matte stone and cloth, restrained highlights, sparse painted wear. No photorealism, no micro-grain, no dense tiny cracks, no elaborate miniature carvings, no harsh specular highlights.

THE WARLORD: about 4.2 m tall, more than twice a man's height, forged from cooled volcanic basalt and molten copper in a fire god's foundry deep inside a volcano. Proportions: very broad shoulders and a deep barrel chest, big armoured gauntlets and blocky sabatons, sturdy legs, a compact head inside a big horned helm; heavy, grounded and powerful, built for swinging a huge sword with both hands.
- ARMOUR (main colour block, about 60%): thick plates of dark PURPLE-GREY BASALT, matte and stone-like with a cool violet tint, a few big bevelled facets. Chinese general's armour simplified into big readable plates: layered shoulder guards of three big overlapping curved plates on each side flaring outward, a broad chest plate, a segmented waist, a knee-length skirt of overlapping rectangular tasset plates split into front, back and side panels, heavy vambraces, greaves with round knee guards, blocky sabatons.
- COPPER (second colour block, about 25%): warm burnished copper-orange trims along the plate edges, big round copper rivets, a simple meander band on the trims, a simplified beast-face boss on the belt buckle and one on each shoulder guard, a pair of large copper horns on the helm sweeping up and back like a crescent.
- MOLTEN COPPER LINES (accent): a few bold bright-orange molten lines painted in the seams between the armour plates (bold lines, never dense cracks), the slit eyes of the face mask, and the HEART: in the middle of the chest a round furnace-heart, a copper ring frame holding a molten orange core.
- INK (small accent, about 10%): ink-black cloth: a long front tabard and a back tabard hanging from the belt between the tasset plates to below the knees, each split into two broad strips with squared notched ends; ink-black tassels hanging from the helm crest and from each shoulder guard; dark leather straps.
- HELM AND FACE: a big general's helm with the copper crescent horns and a raised crest ridge, a stern demon face mask of basalt with a heavy brow and a closed fanged mouth, glowing orange slit eyes.
- HANDS: huge armoured gauntlets, each with a separate thumb and four plated fingers.

THE GREATSWORD (his weapon): a colossal straight double-edged Chinese greatsword about 3 m long: a broad blade of dark purple-grey basalt-steel with burnished copper edges, a raised spine down the middle carrying one bold molten-orange cloud-scroll line, a heavy copper guard shaped like a simplified beast face with two short downward-curved horns, a long two-handed grip wrapped in ink-black cord, a round copper ring pommel with a short ink-black tassel.

Palette: dark violet basalt, burnished copper, molten orange accents, a little ink black. NO red cape, NO red cloth, NO flames growing from the body, NO fur, NO mane, NO skulls.

LAYOUT: plain flat light grey background (#D6D6D6) everywhere. Left half: one large three-quarter view of the warlord in a powerful sword stance, gripping the greatsword in BOTH hands raised over his right shoulder ready to cleave. Right half, at the same scale: a FRONT view and a BACK view of the warlord standing straight in a relaxed A-pose with EMPTY open hands, and the greatsword alone standing upright on its tip with its flat face toward the viewer. Everything complete with margin. Absolutely NO text, NO letters, NO titles, NO labels, NO colour swatches, NO panels, NO frames, NO scenery, NO extra small pictures. Even studio lighting, no cast shadows.
