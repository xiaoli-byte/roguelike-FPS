Chapter 3 'Zhurong's Fire Prison' (祝融火狱): the underground forge-palace and prison of Zhurong (祝融), the fire god of Chinese myth, deep under a volcano: black basalt pillars, black-iron halls, giant bronze ding cauldrons and furnaces, hanging chains, fire altars and rivers of lava. The god's fire has run wild and turned his smiths, jailers, forge-made constructs and fire beasts into fire fiends.
