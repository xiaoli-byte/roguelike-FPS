Original PLAYER HERO design for a polished STYLIZED 3D first-person roguelite shooter, matching the hero art of Gunfire Reborn. Strongly stylized, not realistic: heroic chibi-leaning proportions about 4.5 heads tall (big expressive animal head, oversized hands and boots, compact sturdy body), bold readable silhouette, smooth sculpted forms with clean bevelled edges, fur as a few large carved clumps (never individual strands), clothing as thick simple folds, flat-ish hand-painted textures with soft gradients and restrained edge wear, two or three dominant colour blocks plus one glowing spirit-fire accent. Likeable, confident, a little cheeky: this is the character the player chooses. No photorealistic fur, skin, anatomy detail, realistic fabric or semi-realistic painting. Distinctly Chinese mythological fantasy mixed with adventurer gear (holsters, belts, pouches).

The heroes are wandering spirit-fire wardens (灵火守望者) who carry guns powered by spirit fire through the Red Sand Silk Road, the Kunlun Snow Ruins and Zhurong's Fire Hell. Their species, silhouettes and palettes must stay clearly apart from the enemy roster (jerboa, scorpion archer, toad, apsara, fire rat, lion-like tomb beast, white nine-tailed fox witch, blue bird, ape, black tortoise).

Character: 燎 (red panda fire gunslinger), about 1.55 m tall. An agile anthropomorphic RED PANDA gunslinger and firework grenadier. Round head with small rounded ears with white rims, white cheek and eyebrow markings and tear-stripes, bright amber eyes, a cheeky grin. Rust-red fur on head and back, black legs and arms, a huge bushy tail with bold cream and rust rings. Wears a short dark-charcoal Song-dynasty martial jacket with a high collar and ember-orange lining, the right sleeve rolled up, a cinnabar sash, wrapped black trousers and sturdy short boots. A diagonal bandolier of red Chinese firecracker grenades (爆竹手雷) with gold paper caps across the chest, a small brass fire-starter lantern (火折子) on the hip glowing with orange-gold spirit fire. Hands with a clearly separate thumb and four separate fingers.

Output: one character design sheet on a plain flat light grey background (#D6D6D6), no text, labels or frames.
