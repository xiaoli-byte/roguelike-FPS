Original PLAYER HERO design for a polished STYLIZED 3D first-person roguelite shooter, matching the hero art of Gunfire Reborn. Strongly stylized, not realistic: heroic chibi-leaning proportions about 4.5 heads tall (big expressive animal head, oversized hands and boots, compact sturdy body), bold readable silhouette, smooth sculpted forms with clean bevelled edges, fur as a few large carved clumps (never individual strands), clothing as thick simple folds, flat-ish hand-painted textures with soft gradients and restrained edge wear, two or three dominant colour blocks plus one glowing spirit-fire accent. Likeable, confident, a little cheeky: this is the character the player chooses. No photorealistic fur, skin, anatomy detail, realistic fabric or semi-realistic painting. Distinctly Chinese mythological fantasy mixed with adventurer gear (holsters, belts, pouches).

The heroes are wandering spirit-fire wardens (灵火守望者) who carry guns powered by spirit fire through the Red Sand Silk Road, the Kunlun Snow Ruins and Zhurong's Fire Hell. Their species, silhouettes and palettes must stay clearly apart from the enemy roster (jerboa, scorpion archer, toad, apsara, fire rat, lion-like tomb beast, white nine-tailed fox witch, blue bird, ape, black tortoise).

Character: 鼯 (giant flying squirrel thunder scout), about 1.6 m tall. A nimble anthropomorphic Chinese GIANT FLYING SQUIRREL (鼯鼠) thunder scout and Mohist gadgeteer. Big dark glossy eyes with a white eye ring, short rounded ears, a short muzzle, chestnut-brown fur with a cream belly and face, a long flat fluffy tail. A furred gliding membrane runs from each WRIST down to each ANKLE (never between the fingers), shown as a soft drape like a short cape along the flanks. Wears a leather aviator cap with brass goggles pushed up, an indigo Mohist (墨家) tunic with a utility harness, copper-coil wrist gadgets, and a small thunder-drum (雷鼓) beacon clipped to the belt glowing electric cyan-violet. Soft leather boots. Hands with a clearly separate thumb and four separate fingers, completely free of the membrane.

Output: one character design sheet on a plain flat light grey background (#D6D6D6), no text, labels or frames. Left half: a large three-quarter view in a confident action pose with a long slim bronze rail rifle with copper coils crackling with cyan-violet lightning, mid-glide with the membranes spread wide. Right half, side by side at the same scale: a FRONT view and a BACK view standing in a relaxed A-pose with empty open hands, full body head to feet. Bottom right: two small close-ups, one open hand (thumb plus four separate fingers, clearly countable) and one foot or boot. Even studio lighting, no cast shadows.
