Original character design for a polished stylized 3D first-person roguelite shooter, at the production
quality of Gunfire Reborn enemies: bold readable silhouette, exaggerated characterful proportions, chunky clean forms,
hand-painted PBR textures with soft gradients and restrained edge wear, two or three dominant colour blocks plus one
glowing accent. Distinctly Chinese mythological fantasy. Memorable at 30 metres; one obvious glowing weak spot.

Chapter 1 'Red Sand Silk Road' (赤沙古道): a Silk Road oasis city swallowed by quicksand, where Ba the drought goddess (旱魃) has awoken and turned the city's people, beasts and statues into sand fiends. Chinese mythology (Shan Hai Jing) and Dunhuang cave art. Palette: ochre-red sandstone, faded Dunhuang mural colours (malachite green, cinnabar red, lapis blue), old gold; the corrupting fire is a blazing white-gold sun fire with heat shimmer.

Character: 旱魃, about 5 m tall. Chapter boss Ba the drought goddess (旱魃), who 'wears green clothes' in the Shan Hai Jing: a towering, gaunt, unsettlingly graceful goddess with very long limbs, ash-grey sun-cracked skin, long white hair floating upward in the heat, a calm terrifying face with eyes of blazing white-gold sun fire. Tattered layered robes of faded jade-green and teal (青衣) with old gold Dunhuang borders, cracked jade ornaments, a huge burning sun ring (日轮) floating behind her back, a glowing sun core in her chest (weak spot). Bare feet with five toes standing on cracked dry earth that splits around her. Long hands with a clearly separate thumb and four long fingers.
