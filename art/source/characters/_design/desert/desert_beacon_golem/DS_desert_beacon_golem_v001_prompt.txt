Distinctly Chinese mythological fantasy. Memorable at 30 metres; one obvious glowing weak spot.

Chapter 1 'Red Sand Silk Road' (赤沙古道): a Silk Road oasis city swallowed by quicksand, where Ba the drought goddess (旱魃) has awoken and turned the city's people, beasts and statues into sand fiends. Chinese mythology (Shan Hai Jing) and Dunhuang cave art. Palette: ochre-red sandstone, faded Dunhuang mural colours (malachite green, cinnabar red, lapis blue), old gold; the corrupting fire is a blazing white-gold sun fire with heat shimmer.

Character: 烽燧夯士, about 2.8 m tall. A Han-dynasty Great Wall beacon tower (烽燧) from the Yumen Pass, risen as a walking giant: a squat, top-heavy golem whose body is the beacon tower itself, built of horizontal layers of rammed earth (夯土) striped with thin layers of reed and tamarisk branches, old wooden beam ends poking out. No neck: a small stern face carved into the front of the tower top, under a battlement crown. Huge rammed-earth forearms like two short walls that it raises and crosses in front of its face to guard, big blocky three-fingered hands plus thumb. Short thick legs of stacked earth. On its back a bronze beacon fire basin burns with white-gold sun fire (weak spot). Faded red Han banners tied to its shoulders.

Stylization: cartoon game proportions like the jerboa soldier and fire rat of this roster: chunky simplified masses, oversized head and paws, toy-like readable forms with clean bevelled edges. Its defence is part of its own body: it holds NO shield and NO weapon.
