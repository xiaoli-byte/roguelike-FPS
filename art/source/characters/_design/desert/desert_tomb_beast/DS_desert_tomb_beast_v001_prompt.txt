Original character design for a polished STYLIZED 3D first-person roguelite shooter, at the production
quality of Gunfire Reborn enemies. Strongly stylized, not realistic: simplified chunky shapes, exaggerated cartoon-like
proportions (big head and hands, compact body), bold readable silhouette, smooth sculpted forms with clean bevelled
edges, hair and fur as a few large carved clumps (never individual strands), clothing as thick simple folds, flat-ish
hand-painted textures with soft gradients and restrained edge wear, two or three dominant colour blocks plus one
glowing accent. No photorealistic skin, pores, anatomy detail, realistic fabric or semi-realistic painting.
Distinctly Chinese mythological fantasy. Memorable at 30 metres; one obvious glowing weak spot.

Chapter 1 'Red Sand Silk Road' (赤沙古道): a Silk Road oasis city swallowed by quicksand, where Ba the drought goddess (旱魃) has awoken and turned the city's people, beasts and statues into sand fiends. Chinese mythology (Shan Hai Jing) and Dunhuang cave art. Palette: ochre-red sandstone, faded Dunhuang mural colours (malachite green, cinnabar red, lapis blue), old gold; the corrupting fire is a blazing white-gold sun fire with heat shimmer.

Character: 三彩镇墓兽, about 2.2 m tall. A Tang-dynasty sancai-glazed tomb guardian beast (镇墓兽) from the Silk Road tombs, awakened by the drought goddess: a hulking quadruped lion-like beast built of hollow glazed ceramic, sitting heavy and low. A fierce human-beast face mask with bulging eyes, a single tall curved horn and flame-shaped ear crests; big flame-like sculpted wing fins on the shoulders; thick stubby legs ending in big three-clawed feet. Its body is glazed in the three sancai colours: amber-brown, malachite green and cream, with glaze drips running down. The front (face mask, chest and forelegs) is extra-thick glossy glaze armour. A big jagged crack across its back splits the ceramic open, white-gold sun fire glowing inside the hollow body (weak spot). Tank: lowers its head and horn to guard and charge.

Stylization: cartoon game proportions like the jerboa soldier and fire rat of this roster: chunky simplified masses, oversized head and paws, toy-like readable forms with clean bevelled edges. Its defence is part of its own body: it holds NO shield and NO weapon.

Output: one creature design sheet on a plain flat light grey background (#D6D6D6), no text, labels or
frames. Left half: a large three-quarter view in an aggressive action pose. Right half at the same scale: a FRONT view,
a SIDE view and a BACK view, each complete with margin. Even studio lighting, no cast shadows.
